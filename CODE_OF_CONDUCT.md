# Code of conduct

Taskrivet welcomes contributors and users with different backgrounds, experience,
and perspectives. These expectations apply to repository issues, pull requests,
reviews, and discussions, and to anyone representing the project.

## Expected conduct

- Be respectful, specific, and patient. Critique the work rather than the person.
- Assume incomplete context before assuming bad intent; ask for evidence and clarify.
- Respect privacy and consent. Share only material you have permission to publish.
- Make room for newcomers and people with different accessibility needs.
- Disclose uncertainty and correct mistakes. Give credit for others' contributions.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexual harassment, doxxing, impersonation,
spam, and repeated personal attacks are unacceptable. Do not publish another
person's private information or target them outside the project.

## Reporting and enforcement

Use GitHub's **Report content** or **Report abuse** controls for private reports of
abuse. See [GitHub's reporting guide](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam).
Do not post sensitive allegations or personal information in a public issue.
For non-sensitive moderation questions, contact the repository maintainer through
a discussion without naming or exposing the affected person.

Maintainers may clarify expectations, edit or remove content, lock a discussion,
or restrict participation in response to violations. Responses should consider
the behavior, its impact, and whether it is repeated. Reports should be handled
respectfully, with private details shared only when necessary to investigate.

These project expectations supplement GitHub's own community guidelines and
platform reporting processes.
